🎀 Canva for Beginners

Lesson 1: Start with Canva

1. Open Canva and sign in.
2. Choose a design type, such as a poster.
3. Select a blank page or a ready-made design.
4. Add text, photos and elements.
5. Download your finished design.

📱 Instagram Designs

How to make an Instagram post

1. Open Canva and search Instagram Post.
2. Choose a layout you like.
3. Change the text, colors and pictures.
4. Add your own style and save your design.

💡 Canva Editing Tips

Useful tricks

• Use matching colors for a neat design.
• Use two fonts instead of too many.
• Keep enough empty space around text.
• Use Position to arrange elements.
• Use transparency for soft effects.

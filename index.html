✨ Editing Tips & Tricks

Small tips that can make your designs look better.

💙 Tip 1: Choose matching colors

Use two or three matching colors to keep your design clean and attractive.

🌷 Tip 2: Use readable fonts

Choose simple fonts and avoid using too many different font styles.

🎨 Tip 3: Use good spacing

Leave enough space between text, images, and other design elements.

✨ Tip 4: Try Canva elements

Explore shapes, icons, stickers, and frames to make your designs creative.
